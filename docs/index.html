Paani Glass – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026 · App package: com.paaniglass.app

Summary

Paani Glass is a simple water-intake tracker. It does not collect, send or share any personal data. Everything you log stays on your own device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account, no sign-up and no servers. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers or usage data.

Information stored on your device

  • The number of glasses you log each day
  • Your daily goal and chosen glass size

This data is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded anywhere and is not included in cloud backups. Uninstalling the app, or using "Clear data" in Android settings, deletes it permanently.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It does not use the internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage or notifications.

Ads, analytics and third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs. No data is shared with or sold to anyone.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Health notice

Paani Glass is a habit tool to help you remember to drink water. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Follow your doctor's guidance about how much water is right for you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the developer contact email shown on the Paani Glass page in the Google Play Store.
