```
Paani Glass - Water Tracker
```

**Short description** (max 80):
```
Tap the glass to log water, set a daily goal and see your 7-day progress.
```

**Full description** (English):
```
Paani Glass is a simple, friendly way to keep track of how much water you drink each day.

Tap the glass and watch it fill up. Every tap logs one glass, and a progress bar shows how close you are to your daily goal.

FEATURES
• Animated glass that fills as you drink
• Set your own daily goal (4 to 16 glasses)
• Choose your glass size: 150, 200, 250, 300 or 500 ml
• Undo a glass if you tapped by mistake
• 7-day chart with your goal line
• Streak, daily average and weekly total in litres
• Light and dark theme

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up
• No internet permission, no ads, no tracking
• Your data stays only on your phone

Paani Glass is a habit tool to help you remember to drink water. It does not give medical advice. If you have a health condition, follow your doctor's guidance about water intake.
```
