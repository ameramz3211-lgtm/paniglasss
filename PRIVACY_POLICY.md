# Privacy Policy — Paani Glass

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Paani Glass is a simple water-intake tracker.

**Data we collect:** None. The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

**Data on your device:** The number of glasses you log each day, your daily goal and glass size are saved only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

**Permissions:** The app requests no permissions. It does not use the internet, location, camera, contacts or storage.

**Ads and analytics:** The app contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

**Children:** The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

**Medical note:** Paani Glass is a habit tool only and does not provide medical advice.
